07 · Deep Dive

Claude Analytics & the Membership Insights Report

The Challenge
Amplitude had the events, but nobody could answer who converts, who renews and why without hours of manual digging.
Our Solution
Ed connected Claude to Amplitude via MCP and added per-feature context docs so every answer is grounded in how the app actually works. The output is a recurring Membership Insights report — №4 published September 25.
What We Learned
1,984 monthly active users and 94 paying actives. Renewers are in the app on 50% of paid days vs. 36% for churners, and the Throwing Program is the anchor feature (93% of renewers use it).
Next
Free AA-trial conversion metrics, Rookie activation experiments, and exposing athlete goals & feedback to Claude once the Python upgrade lands.
Results
Decisions backed by data · weekly answers for Trevor in minutes · one shared source of truth for product, marketing and the client.
